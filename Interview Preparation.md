## How to practice

For each topic:

1. Explain the concept in your own words, including when it is useful.
2. Write or walk through a small example.
3. Compare alternatives and describe their trade-offs.
4. Relate it to a real project, including a mistake or limitation you encountered.
5. Review the relevant official documentation before relying on a memorized answer.

For backend roles, practice discussing API design, data modeling, transactions, validation, error handling, security, testing, and deployment—not only definitions.
